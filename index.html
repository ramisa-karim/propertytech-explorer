Exploring the intersection of real estate, technology and Web3.

Why I'm interested

I'm studying Real Estate Management and I'm interested in how emerging technologies could change the way we interact with property, investment and ownership.

Blockchain

Blockchain is a type of digital ledger that records transactions across a network. I'm interested in how this technology could potentially improve transparency and the way information about assets is recorded and shared.

Tokenisation

Tokenisation involves representing an asset or an interest in an asset digitally. In real estate, I'm curious about whether this could create new ways of representing ownership and accessing investment opportunities.

How could tokenisation work?

Here's a simplified hypothetical example.

£1,000,000 Commercial Property
10,000 hypothetical tokens
£100 per token

In this simplified example, a £1 million property is represented by 10,000 digital units. Each unit would represent a hypothetical £100 share of the property's total value.


This is only an educational example. Real-world tokenisation involves legal, regulatory, ownership and valuation considerations.

Smart Contracts

Smart contracts are programs that can automatically execute actions when predefined conditions are met. I'm interested in how this could potentially reduce friction in parts of property transactions.
