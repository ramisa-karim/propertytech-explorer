Why I'm interested

I'm studying Real Estate Management and I'm interested in how emerging technologies could change the way we interact with property, investment and ownership.

Blockchain

Blockchain is a type of digital ledger that records transactions across a network. I'm interested in how this technology could potentially improve transparency and the way information about assets is recorded and shared.

Tokenisation

Tokenisation involves representing an asset or an interest in an asset digitally. In real estate, I'm curious about whether this could make certain forms of property investment more accessible and create new ways of representing ownership.

Smart Contracts

Smart contracts are programs that can automatically execute actions when predefined conditions are met. I'm interested in how this could potentially reduce friction in parts of property transactions.
