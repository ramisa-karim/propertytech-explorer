Welcome to my project

I'm studying Real Estate Management and exploring how technology could shape the future of property. This project is where I document what I'm learning about PropTech, blockchain and digital assets.

I'm particularly interested in commercial real estate and how technology could influence investment, ownership and asset management.

Why I'm interested

I'm interested in how emerging technologies could change the way we interact with property, investment and ownership. By combining my real estate studies with coding and technology, I want to understand how these ideas could apply to real-world industries.

01 / Blockchain

Blockchain is a type of digital ledger that records transactions across a network. I'm interested in how this technology could potentially improve transparency and the way information about assets is recorded and shared.

02 / Tokenisation

Tokenisation involves representing an asset, or an interest in an asset, digitally. In real estate, I'm curious about whether this could create new ways of representing ownership and accessing investment opportunities.
