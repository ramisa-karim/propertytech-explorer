03 / THE TECHNOLOGY

What is blockchain?

Blockchain is a digital record system that stores information in connected blocks. It can help networks maintain and verify transaction records without relying on a single central record.

In real estate, potential applications include transaction records, ownership documentation and digital representations of certain property-related rights.

04 / DIGITAL PROPERTY

What is property tokenisation?

Tokenisation involves representing an asset or rights connected to it as digital tokens. In property, the rights attached to tokens depend on the legal and investment structure.

Breaking down the example

£1,000,000 divided by 10,000 equal units gives a notional value of £100 per unit. For example, five units represent £500 of notional value in this simplified illustration.

In a real arrangement, tokens do not automatically confer legal ownership of part of a building. Actual rights, costs, risks and returns depend on the investment structure.

06 / AUTOMATION

Smart contracts and real estate

Smart contracts are programs on a blockchain that can execute predefined actions when specified conditions are met. They could potentially automate certain steps in property-related transactions, but legal agreements and reliable information remain essential.
